Interview Highlights

Q: What's the difference between a researcher and a storyteller?
"A researcher says: here is the evidence. A storyteller says: here is the evidence — now let me show you what it means and why you should care. My job is to be both. I never compromise the evidence. But I shape it for maximum impact."
Q: How do you turn Grade C into compelling content?
"You lead with the uncertainty. Don't say 'the soul trap is real.' Say 'what if the NDE tunnel is not heaven but a technological recycling system?' A Grade C track becomes compelling not because it's certain — but because it's possible. And possibility, when handled honestly, is more powerful than certainty."
