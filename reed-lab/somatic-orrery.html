The Somatic Trifecta GPS

Three real-time proprioceptive protocols. No visualization — pure mechanical readout. 5-second audit.

1. SPIN — Heart Torus Phase-Shift

Test: Right palm on sternum, left palm on mid-back. Breathe in 4 seconds through nose.
Track: Does sternum expand first, back follows a microsecond later? That phase lag IS the toroidal spin.
✔ Coherent: Front-then-back wave. Subtle thrum/buzz in sternum. Armpits widen.
✘ Stalled: Both expand simultaneously (2D breathing). Fix: inhale into back of heart for 3 breaths.

2. TILT — Iliac Crest Plumb

Test: Stand, eyes closed. Thumbs on iliac crests (hip bones). Fingers on flank muscles (between lowest ribs and hip).
Track: Which thumb is higher? Which flank (QL muscle) is gripping?
✔ Straight: Equal weight balls-to-heels. Both flanks equally soft and quiet.
✘ Tilted: One QL gripping = C-curve tilt. Fix: shift 5% weight to opposite heel until sacrum "clicks."

3. MOON — Cranial Fluid Drain

Test: Index fingers on cheekbones, thumbs on temples. Drop jaw, relax tongue to floor of mouth.
Track: Cheekbone temperature. Eye socket pressure direction.
✔ Calm: Cool cheekbones, heavy. Eyes sink backward onto cushion of fluid. Cool behind nasal cavity.
✘ Hijacked: Hot cheekbones, twitchy temples, eyes bulging forward. Fix: tongue to roof of mouth 5 sec → release with "Tsk."
