# Architect Pulse

## Product Purpose
Personal life-channel tracking dashboard. Monitors 11 blocked channels across Paul's life, showing movement, stagnation, streaks, and emergence score. The most important screen in the Cathedral system — the one Paul opens daily.

## Register
product

## Users
Single user: Paul. Boxing gym owner, Hong Kong. Visual thinker. Drawn to premium, dark, cinematic aesthetics. Opens this on desktop (Mac Mini) and phone. Needs to feel pulled toward it, not obligated.

## Brand / Tone
- Cathedral system: sovereign, private, intimate
- Not corporate. Not gamified. Not wellness-pastel.
- Think: premium trading terminal + meditation app + health dashboard
- Dark, warm, alive. Not cold dark-mode-for-the-sake-of-it.
- The gym at night with one amber lamp. Hong Kong city lights out of focus.

## Anti-references
- Habit tracker apps (streaks as punishment, gamification badges)
- Generic SaaS dashboards (white cards, blue accents, rounded everything)
- Wellness apps (pastel, soft, patronizing)
- Notion/productivity tools (flat, utilitarian, no soul)

## Strategic Principles
- Gravitation over obligation: the screen should pull you in
- Alive not static: subtle motion indicates the system is watching
- Warmth in darkness: amber/gold tones, not cold blue
- Zoom-in and zoom-out: shows bottleneck AND bigger picture simultaneously
- Zero shame: red channels are information, not punishment
- The architecture breathes: active channels glow, dormant ones rest
