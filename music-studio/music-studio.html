Producer Tips

  • Use [verse], [chorus], [bridge], [outro] tags in lyrics for song structure
  • Leave lyrics empty for instrumental tracks
  • Higher steps = better quality but slower (100 is solid, 200 for finals)
  • Guidance scale: 10-15 for balanced, 20+ for more prompt-adherent
  • Seed: save the seed of tracks you like to reproduce similar results
  • Duration: 30s for loops/samples, 60s for short tracks, 120-180s for full songs
  • Combine genres: "cuban son meets UK drill" works surprisingly well
  • For boxing content: "aggressive", "high energy", "pump up", "walk-in anthem"
