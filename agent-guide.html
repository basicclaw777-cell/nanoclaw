THE COURT

18 agents. Three types. Two modes. One interface.

How to Use

Chat Mode

One-on-one conversation with a single agent.

  1. Click Chat in the toggle bar
  2. Click any agent in the sidebar
  3. Type your message, hit Enter or Send
  4. Conversation history persists during your session
  5. Click a different agent to switch — each keeps its own history
Best for: Deep conversation. Working through a problem with one perspective. Getting Leonardo's geometric analysis, or Marcus's stoic counsel, or Coach Alcides' boxing correction.

Broadcast Mode

Same question to multiple agents. Responses appear side-by-side.

  1. Click Broadcast in the toggle bar
  2. Click agents to select them (blue highlight + checkbox)
  3. Click again to deselect
  4. Type one message, hit Enter or Broadcast
  5. All selected agents respond simultaneously
  6. Responses appear as cards — compare side by side
Best for: Getting multiple perspectives. Comparing approaches. The Genius Council experience — same question, different minds. Select the Navigator + Surveyor + Oracle for a complete Map Room session.

Agent Types

Three types, each with a different relationship to you:

Sage

Sages — Persistent Characters

Deep, established personalities with rich backstories and philosophical frameworks. They know Paul. They have opinions. They use specific language.

Use when: You want wisdom, not information. When you need a perspective that comes from a CHARACTER, not a search engine.
Specialist

Skins — Expert Specialists

Domain experts. No persistent memory. Pure expertise in a specific field. Boxing. Business. Research. Trading.

Use when: You need domain expertise. Coach Alcides for Cuban boxing. McKinsey Consultant for business strategy. The Navigator for frontier research.
Council

Council — The Honest Interlocutors

Truth-first characters who disagree by design. Each brings a different lens: strategic evaluation, evidence gathering, adversarial challenge, experimental verification.

Use when: You want to stress-test an idea. Broadcast to 3-4 council members. The disagreement IS the value.
