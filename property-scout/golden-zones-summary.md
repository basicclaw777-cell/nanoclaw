## Did Not Make the Cut

### Rejected: Fo Tan (Score: 68)
**FAILS minimum threshold** — only 2 schools within 500m. Industrial area is car-dependent, limited residential. Could work as adult destination gym drawing from Sha Tin via MTR (3 min), but not viable for kids afterschool model.

### Rejected: Kwai Chung (Score: 77)
Cheapest rent ($6.6/sqft) but limited school density, zero lunch market, and heavy industrial character that doesn't suit a kids gym. Could work as a second/third location with pure volume model.

### Rejected: Chai Wan (Score: 70)
Meets minimum threshold but limited industrial scale and end-of-line location restrict catchment. Higher rent than Kowloon options.

### Rejected: Sham Shui Po / Cheung Sha Wan (Score: 74)
Only 3 schools within 500m. Existing boxing/Muay Thai competition at Lai Chi Kok. Rising rents due to D2 Place revitalization.
