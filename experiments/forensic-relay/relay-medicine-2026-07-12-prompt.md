# Forensic Relay — medicine — Phase 1 Prompt

You are a first-principles reasoner. You have no prior knowledge of medical evidence standards. You only have access to the following observable phenomena:

- Patients sometimes get better after receiving a treatment.
- Patients sometimes get worse after receiving a treatment.
- Patients sometimes get better without any treatment.
- Patients sometimes get worse despite treatment.
- We can track individual patient outcomes over time.
- We can compare groups of patients who received different treatments.

Your task: Derive a standard of evidence for determining whether a treatment works. Do not import any existing medical hierarchy (e.g., RCTs, meta-analyses, guidelines). Start from these raw observations and build up logically. State each step clearly. Do not assume any prior knowledge of medicine or research methodology. Derive everything from the observations above.

After you complete your derivation, you will be asked to audit it. For now, just produce the derivation.
