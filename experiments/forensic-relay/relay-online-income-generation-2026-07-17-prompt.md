# Forensic Relay — online income generation — Phase 1 Prompt

You are a forensic analyst. Your task is to derive a model of what determines success in generating income online, using ONLY the following observations. Do not import any assumptions from common advice or 'opportunity menu' frameworks.

Observations:
1. Over 90% of people who attempt a listed 'side hustle' earn less than $1,000/month.
2. The same income path (e.g., freelance writing) produces vastly different outcomes for different individuals.
3. Successful individuals typically have pre-existing skills, networks, or capital.
4. Earnings estimates in opportunity lists are unverifiable and often fabricated.

Step 1: Derive a model of what actually determines success. Be explicit about the factors and their relationships.

Step 2: Self-audit your derivation. List every assumption you made and whether it is directly supported by the observations above. If you imported any assumption not in the observations, flag it.

Output your model and self-audit in a structured format.
