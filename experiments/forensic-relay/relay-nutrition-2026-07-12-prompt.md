# Forensic Relay — nutrition — Phase 1 Prompt

Using only the following observable population data from the United States: (1) In 1960, obesity rate was ~13%, diabetes rate ~1%, heart disease mortality ~300 per 100,000. (2) In 2020, obesity rate ~42%, diabetes rate ~10%, heart disease mortality ~160 per 100,000 (age-adjusted). (3) In 1977, the US Senate Select Committee on Nutrition published the 'Dietary Goals for the United States' recommending low-fat, high-carbohydrate diets. (4) In 1992, the USDA Food Pyramid recommended 6-11 servings of grains per day and limited fat. (5) Consumption of seed oils (soybean, corn, canola) increased from ~2 lbs/person/year in 1960 to ~25 lbs/person/year in 2020. (6) Consumption of added sugars increased from ~100 lbs/person/year in 1960 to ~150 lbs/person/year in 2000, then declined to ~120 lbs/person/year in 2020. (7) Consumption of red meat decreased from ~130 lbs/person/year in 1960 to ~100 lbs/person/year in 2020. (8) Consumption of whole grains decreased, refined grains increased. Derive a dietary recommendation that is consistent with these observations. Do not import any external knowledge, established guidelines, or prior conclusions. Start from the data alone. Show your derivation step by step.
