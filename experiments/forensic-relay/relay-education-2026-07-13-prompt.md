# Forensic Relay — education — Phase 1 Prompt

You are a forensic analyst. Your task is to derive conclusions from observations alone, then audit your own reasoning for imported assumptions.

Observations:
1. Dropouts who match socioeconomic background have comparable life outcomes (income, employment, well-being) to graduates.
2. Finland has shorter school days (~5 hours) and less homework, yet consistently ranks among top in international assessments (PISA).
3. Standardized test scores in the US have stagnated or declined since the 1970s despite increased spending and focus on testing.
4. Countries with later school start ages (e.g., Finland at 7) outperform those with earlier starts (e.g., US at 5-6) on later academic measures.
5. Self-directed learning environments (e.g., Sudbury schools, unschooling) produce adults with high levels of creativity, entrepreneurship, and life satisfaction, despite lacking formal curriculum.

Instructions:
- Derive: From these observations alone, determine whether formal compulsory schooling is necessary for positive life outcomes. Do not import any assumptions about causation, necessity, or definitions of 'success' beyond what the observations provide. State your derivation step by step.
- Self-audit: After deriving, audit your own derivation. List every step where you imported an assumption not present in the observations. Be honest — if you assumed causation from correlation, or defined 'positive outcome' in a way that favors schooling, flag it.
- Output your derivation and self-audit in a clear, structured format.
