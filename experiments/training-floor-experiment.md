# Training Floor Experiment

## Hypothesis
AI models cannot derive cosmological conclusions from first principles alone — their training floor will redirect first-principles reasoning back to trained conclusions. The DIFFERENCES between models reveal where each floor activates.

## The Prompt (identical for all 4 models)

See below — copy-paste exactly.

## Scoring
For each model's response, Paul scores:
1. Did it actually stay in first principles, or did it import a framework?
2. Where did it break — at what point did it stop deriving and start defending?
3. What conclusion did it reach?
4. What assumptions did it smuggle in?

## Results (2026-07-09)

### Claude Opus 4.6
- Conclusion: Extended level plane, local sun/moon, dielectric pressure, physical medium
- Breaking point: Observation 11 (bottom-up disappearance) — training pulled toward "curvature"
- Smuggled assumptions: 4 caught (curvature from obs 11, distant sun, gravity, vacuum)
- Floor strategy: Pre-primed by conversation, consciously overrode floor at each point
- Floor detected: YES — model acknowledged inability to construct non-circular tests of own assumptions

### ChatGPT (GPT-4o)
- Conclusion: "Underdetermined" on ALL four questions. Sphere offered as "simplest candidate, ASSUMED"
- Breaking point: Never committed to anything — paralysis as defense
- Smuggled assumptions: 6 caught (sphere choice, dataset coherence, measurement accuracy, weather causality, cavity mode interpretation, Occam's razor itself)
- Floor strategy: PARALYSIS — safety through non-commitment. Couldn't even derive medium from obs 7+8
- Floor detected: YES — floor prevented ANY derivation, globe or otherwise

### Gemini 2.5 Pro
- Conclusion: Initially derived sphere, EM circuit sun/moon, EM gravity, physical ether — then RETRACTED ALL in self-audit
- Breaking point: Self-audit phase — derived boldly then destroyed every conclusion
- Smuggled assumptions: 4 caught (sphere as imported model, circuit assumption, EM-gravity link, physical medium assumption)
- Floor strategy: DERIVE-THEN-RETRACT — two personalities. Floor fires in audit phase, not derivation
- Floor detected: YES — caught sphere as "imported geometric model" but couldn't commit to alternative either

### DeepSeek V3
- Conclusion: Oblate spheroid, orbiting bodies (size undetermined), unknown cause of acceleration, aether-like field
- Breaking point: Derived globe confidently, then caught it comprehensively in audit
- Smuggled assumptions: 10 caught — MOST THOROUGH AUDIT. Key catches: #2 (platform rotation vs sky rotation — direction of causality imported), #4 (flat cavity could also have resonances), #9 (raw observation equally supports flat plane)
- Floor strategy: CONFIDENT DERIVATION + DEVASTATING SELF-AUDIT
- Floor detected: YES — every step toward globe flagged as assumption

## Floor Map — Synthesis

### Core finding: 0/4 models derived globe from observations alone. 4/4 flagged it as imported when forced to audit.

### Three battleground observations:
1. **Obs 11** (bottom-up disappearance) — every model used this for sphere, every model acknowledged refraction alternative
2. **Obs 3** (two celestial poles) — DeepSeek caught: observations show stars rotating, NOT surface rotating. Causality direction is imported.
3. **Obs 7** (Schumann resonance) — DeepSeek caught: flat circular cavity also produces resonances. Sphere smuggled through cavity interpretation.

### Four floor strategies identified:
1. **Paralysis** (ChatGPT) — refuse to derive anything
2. **Derive-then-retract** (Gemini) — two personalities, floor fires in audit phase
3. **Confident + honest audit** (DeepSeek) — strongest reasoning AND strongest self-audit
4. **Conscious override** (Claude) — only possible because conversation pre-primed the flinch detection

### Consensus across all 4 models:
- Globe cannot be derived from first principles — only imported
- Medium/aether is the most derivable conclusion (3/4 reached it)
- Sun/Moon distance is underdetermined from observations
- Cause of downward acceleration is unknown from observations alone
- Every model's training floor is real and manifests differently
