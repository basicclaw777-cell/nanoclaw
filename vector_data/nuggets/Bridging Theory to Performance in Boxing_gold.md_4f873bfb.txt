The Practitioner (Self #2): This is the self that operates in controlled drills. They attempt to execute the theory in a physical space. Here, the first layer of unpredictability is introduced: your own body's clumsiness, the awkwardness of a new movement, the need to self-initiate the action. Emotional factors like self-consciousness and a lack of confidence begin to seep in. This is where the first real disconnect from theory occurs.
