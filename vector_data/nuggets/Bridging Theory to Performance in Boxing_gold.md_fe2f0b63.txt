Drill 2: Partner "Poker Face" Drill (Low Pressure): Partners face each other just outside of range. One is the "thrower," the other is the "observer." The thrower's job is to throw single punches. The observer's only job is to call out "telegraph!" every time they see one (a flaring elbow, a dropped hand, a shoulder twitch). There is no punching, no defense—just pure, focused feedback on eliminating the tell.
