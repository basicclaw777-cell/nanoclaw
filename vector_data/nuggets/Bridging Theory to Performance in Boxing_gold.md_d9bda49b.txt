The Performer: This is where the breakdown occurs. Under the pressure of sparring, the Performer's instincts are flawed. It experiences Sensory and Ego Overload. The incoming opponent, the desire to land a "good" shot, the adrenaline, and the noise all cause tunnel vision. The Performer, panicking, reverts to primal, simplistic goals: "be fast" and "be hard." It abandons the Theorist's complex web of subtle cues because processing them feels too slow under fire.
