The Performer (Self #3): This is the self that exists under pressure—during sparring, one-on-one with a coach, or in a match. This is a completely different state of being. The environment is dynamic and unpredictable. Consequences are real and immediate (e.g., a mistake leads to getting hit). Adrenaline, fatigue, and fear hijack the body and mind. The Performer doesn't have the luxury of calm recall; they must react.
