What This Filters

In: People who want to learn something. Willing to commit a week. Willing to invest $500. They'll be the members who stay.

Out: Window shoppers. One-session tourists. People who want to "try boxing" without trying boxing. They self-select out at the price and the 5-day commitment.

Hidden Wins

Quiet hour. Only beginners — everyone's relaxed, go slow, get to know the characters.

Daily structure for Paul. Each day has a theme. No more "what do I cover with beginners today?" Focus one thing, do it well.

Filming window. Day 1 vs Day 5 footage = the before/after wow factor. Already on Gym Eyes by Friday.

Culture immersion. 5 days in the gym → they know the people, the rhythm, the rules. Conversion to regular member is warm, not cold.
