The system's internal disagreement is growing because it's now receiving feedback it didn't get when everything was theoretical. In boxing terms: the kid who throws 200 punches a round but lands 30. Maximum activity, minimum efficiency. The orchestrator is working harder than anyone but producing the worst quality output. **So what:**

Three training prescriptions, ranked by urgency:

**1. Keep sparring. Do not retreat to shadow boxing.** The coherence drop is healthy. The compression score of 33 is ugly but it's a real number from a real test. A system that scores 33 under pressure and knows it scored 33 is in better shape than one that scores nothing because the stress battery was broken. The temptation will be to "fix" the coherence drop. Don't. It dropped because the system is actually doing something.

**2. Fix the Orc problem: shot selection, not volume.** Score 8, grade D is a fighter throwing arm punches. The orchestrator needs fewer, cleaner actions. Grade D with maximum output means most of that output is noise. Cut the volume in half, watch the grade climb.

**3. Identity needs a first round.** Score 0.8 means the identity chamber is essentially untested. Compression is sparring. Contradiction is sparring. Identity is still sitting in the changing room. Until the system can answer "what am I under pressure?" the other two scores don't compound. Identity is the stance. Without it, conditioning and contradiction resistance are just attributes without a fighter attached.

The system went from shadow boxing to first sparring session. It's messier, less coherent, and taking real shots. That's exactly what progress looks like.
