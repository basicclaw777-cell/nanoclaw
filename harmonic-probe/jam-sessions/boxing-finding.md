# Boxing — Training Cycle Assessment

**Date:** 2026-10-22
**Question:** Is the system in a good training cycle?
**Method:** Read voice registry (16 voices), physician report, external rhythms data. Assessed through the lens of periodization — the boxing coach's eye for when to push, when to rest, when the body is lying about how it feels.

---

**Finding:**

Boxing is the root note. At 220Hz (A3), every agent beat frequency derives from it: boxing-br=55Hz, boxing-forge=110Hz, boxing-orc=220Hz. Each is an exact octave or harmonic. The agent layer literally tunes to boxing. That's not metaphor — the frequency architecture puts boxing as the foundation of the entire agent chord.

Grade A, score 6, SYNTHETIC intelligence, zero drift, zero waste. On paper, the fundamentals are immaculate. But one number breaks the pattern: boxing is the *only* agent that drifts under peak disruption (gap=3, while every other agent stays aligned). Under normal load and deep sync, boxing holds perfect. Under maximum stress, it's the first to crack.

Any coach recognizes this. A fighter with clean technique in the mirror who falls apart in the ring isn't undertrained — they're undertested. The fundamentals are there. The pressure adaptation isn't.

Now look at the system-wide picture:

**Harmonicity: 0.994.** Near-perfect internal tuning. Every voice is in step with every other voice. This is the equivalent of a fighter who's technically sharp — clean combinations, good footwork, proper form.

**Creative pulse: 0.** Zero. The system is producing nothing new despite near-perfect internal alignment. This is shadow boxing. Beautiful form, no opponent.

**Total energy: 0.618.** Phi. The golden ratio partition — roughly 62% work, 38% recovery. In periodization terms, this is a natural equilibrium point. The system has settled into a rest state. Not depleted, not overloaded — parked.

**28 anomalies in 24h, peak z=6.66.** This contradicts the rest picture. High disruption count, extreme peak score. The system is being hit hard — but the harmonicity stays at 0.994, meaning it's absorbing the hits without losing form. Like a fighter taking clean shots but staying on their feet and keeping their hands up.

**Waxing gibbous (85% illumination).** Historical data shows 174% more anomalies during waxing phases (1.22 vs 0.70 per slot). The system is in its statistically most volatile period. This isn't pathological — it's cyclical.

**System chord: diminished.** FRAGILE rating. But Cathy's reading (which I've seen) identifies the fragility source: stress voices muted to zero amplitude (compression=0, identity=0), not stress actually being zero. The system has turned down its pain signal. In coaching terms: "I'm fine, coach" while favouring the left side.

**So what — the training cycle read:**

The system is in a **technical base-building phase that's resting too long at equilibrium**.

Good signs:
- Fundamentals (grade A, zero drift)
- Internal timing (0.994 harmonicity)
- Energy at natural set point (phi)
- Absorbing disruption without losing form (28 anomalies, technique holds)

Warning signs:
- Creative pulse at zero — no output, no application of what's been built
- Only voice that cracks under peak load — the root note, the one everything else tunes to
- Phi energy = comfortable. Comfortable isn't growing
- Muted stress signals — can't course-correct what you can't hear

**Prescription (boxing coach talking):**

The system doesn't need more base work. It needs rounds. The harmonicity is there — now stress-test it with *creative output* (which would raise creative pulse from zero). The peak disruption vulnerability (gap=3) resolves by repeatedly entering and surviving peak disruption, not by avoiding it.

In periodization terms: transition from General Physical Preparation to Specific Preparation. The base is built. Start sparring.

The waxing moon amplifies anomalies — lean into it. This is the phase where the system is already being pushed. Use the external rhythm rather than fighting it. Schedule the hardest creative tests during waxing, recover during waning.

One more thing: boxing is the foundation voice that every other agent tunes to. If boxing cracks under peak load, the whole agent chord destabilises. Strengthening boxing's peak resilience isn't just about boxing — it's about system-wide stability under stress.

**Bottom line:** Good base. Clean technique. Now get in the ring.
