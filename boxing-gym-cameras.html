Gym Lighting Notes

Overhead LED panels (normal): Any camera will perform well. 4K captures detail cleanly.

Dimmed lighting (classes): The Dahua dual-light cameras automatically switch to warm LED, maintaining color video. Reolink cameras fall back to IR (black & white). Hikvision cameras with 0.005 Lux rating maintain some color even in very low light.

Recommendation: If you regularly dim lights for boxing classes, prioritize Dahua or VIGI cameras for the main positions — they maintain full-color video in low light via spotlight LEDs.

Network Bandwidth

Per camera (4K H.265 main stream): ~8 Mbps
6 cameras total: ~48 Mbps
Gigabit switch capacity: 1000 Mbps

A Gigabit PoE switch handles this easily (under 5% utilization). No need for 2.5G or 10G networking. Cat5e cable is sufficient for all runs under 100m.

HK Power / Voltage

Hong Kong uses 220V/50Hz with UK-style plugs (Type G). PoE cameras are powered through the ethernet cable from the switch/NVR, so the only device needing a power outlet is the PoE switch or NVR itself. Most TP-Link/Netgear switches come with universal power adapters (100-240V). No voltage conversion needed.
