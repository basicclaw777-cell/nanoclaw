# Supplier Scout Brief — T-Shirt Printers HK

## Objective
Find 2-3 alternatives to Print House HK for comparison. Paul's rule: "Get 2-3 supplier quotes before committing."

## Current Benchmark (Print House HK)
- DTF printing, 100% cotton
- 60 pcs: HK$94/tee
- 100 pcs: HK$76/tee
- Sample: HK$285
- Kids sizes available, same price
- Turnaround: 7-14 days samples, 14-21 days bulk
- No minimum order
- Location: Wong Chuk Hang

## What to Compare
For each supplier, get:
1. **Name, website, location in HK**
2. **Capabilities**: DTF, DTG, screen print, embroidery?
3. **Pricing at 20/40/60/100 pcs** (sliding scale)
4. **Sample cost and turnaround**
5. **Material options** (must do 100% cotton)
6. **Kids sizes?** (same price or different?)
7. **Maximum print area** (poster tees need large back print, A3+)
8. **Quality** — request 1 sample before any bulk order
9. **Box packaging option** (for limited editions)

1. Do you offer DTF printing on 100% cotton tees?
2. Pricing for 60 pcs, A3 full-colour back print + small front logo?
3. Do you have kids sizes? Same price?
4. Can you do box packaging for limited editions?
5. Sample turnaround and cost?
6. Minimum order?
