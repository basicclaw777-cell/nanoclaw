Channel Brief
The most critical business metric — converting first visits to second visits. CRM audit revealed the gap: median follow-ups = 0. New members walk in, have a great session, then hear nothing. AI follow-up chain closes that gap — the right message at the right time. This is where Cathedral intelligence directly generates revenue.
The Finding: BR CRM shows median follow-up count of zero for new visitors. Every unconverted first visit is lost revenue. A single well-timed message can be worth $500+ in lifetime membership value.
Potential Missions
Follow-Up Chain Optimization
Build and refine the AI follow-up sequence. Message 1 within 2 hours, Message 2 at 48 hours, Message 3 at 7 days. Each one personalized from session notes.
Gain: Automated 1st-to-2nd visit pipeline
Welcome Sequence Testing
A/B test welcome message variations. Tone (warm vs professional), timing (same day vs next morning), channel (WhatsApp vs email). Measure conversion delta.
Gain: Data-driven message templates that actually convert
CRM Data Hygiene

Gain: Actionable contact list — every record reachable
Conversion Rate Tracking
Build the dashboard: first visits per week, conversion to second visit, conversion to membership. Track over time. Measure what the follow-up chain changes.
Gain: Visible conversion funnel with trend data
Lapsed Member Re-engagement
