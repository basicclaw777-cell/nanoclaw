Fear & Greed Index (0-100)

Measures the overall mood of the crypto market. Based on: volatility, volume, social media, surveys, Bitcoin dominance.

0-25: Extreme Fear — Everyone is panicking. Historically the BEST time to buy. Blood in the streets. This is where money is made.
25-40: Fear — Market nervous. Opportunities forming. Watch for signals.
40-60: Neutral — No strong edge. Wait. Don't force trades. Patience is a position.
60-75: Greed — Market confident. Ride existing wins. Don't start new positions.
75-100: Extreme Greed — Euphoria. Everyone's a genius. Historically the WORST time to buy. Consider taking profit.
Boxing parallel: When everyone is rushing forward (greed), they're exposed. When everyone is backing up (fear), they've created space. You step into the space they abandoned. Contrarian = counter-puncher.

The Bull vs Bear Debate

Two AI agents argue before any trade decision. Bull makes the strongest case FOR. Bear makes the strongest case AGAINST. A Trader agent reads both and decides: BUY, SKIP, or WAIT.

How to Read It

Bull case: What could go right. The opportunity. The upside.

Bear case: What could go wrong. The risk. The downside.

Decision: The synthesis. Often the most useful learning tool.

What to Watch For

  • BUY — Bull significantly stronger. Clear edge. But STILL needs to pass the validator.
  • SKIP — Bear wins, or edge too thin. No shame in skipping. Most days you skip.
  • WAIT — Thesis valid, timing wrong. Revisit tomorrow.
Boxing parallel: This is the Genius Council applied to trading. Disagreement by design. The purpose is NOT to always trade — it's to only trade when the case is overwhelming. Most rounds you jab and observe. The cross comes rarely and decisively.

Scale

Each phase must EARN the right to the next. Promotion requires:

  • 20+ trades completed
  • 55%+ win rate
  • Profit factor 1.3+ (wins are 30% bigger than losses on average)
  • Max drawdown < 15% (worst losing streak doesn't kill you)
  • 14+ days (no lucky streaks — prove consistency over time)
Boxing parallel: "Don't throw the cross until you've established the jab." You don't get to trade $1,000 until you've PROVEN it works at $50. The capital increase is the cross — earned through the jab (paper) → testing (micro) → consistency (small). No skipping phases.

Your Daily Practice (Phase 0)

  1. Open Trading Hub — or type /trade on Telegram (30 seconds)
  2. Read three numbers: Fear/Greed + Sentiment + Top Signal
  3. Ask yourself: "Would I buy anything today? Why or why not?"
  4. If yes → read the debate. Do you agree with the decision?
  5. Log your own call — even just mentally: "I would have bought ETH today"
  6. Check next day: Were you right? Would it have worked?
  7. Repeat 20 times. Then assess: are you right more than half the time?

That's it. 5 minutes/day. You're not trading yet. You're building JUDGMENT. The system teaches you to read the market the same way the ring teaches principles — through repetition and attention.

When Things Go Wrong

Never do these:
  • Trade money you need for rent, gym, or family
  • Override the validator because "this one feels different"
  • Skip the stop loss
  • Increase position size after a loss (revenge trading)
  • Trade when emotional, tired, or after drinking
  • Jump phases because you had a lucky streak
The wound that triggers bad trades: "I'm not enough / I need to prove myself." This is the same wound that makes you over-commit to staff who don't reciprocate. In trading it looks like: increasing size after losses, skipping the validator, trading to "make back" what was lost. The jab protocol is the defense. Match what the market gives you. Don't escalate unreciprocated.
