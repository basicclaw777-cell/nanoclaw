Research Notes

Statistical Arbitrage / Pairs Trading (HK) Signal Only
2026-10-14 — Source: DeepSeek forensic audit

Strategy: Trade the spread between two correlated stocks (e.g. HSBC/Hang Seng Bank, A-H dual-listed shares) when they diverge, betting on mean reversion.

Mechanical test result: FAIL for trading. HK market averages 0.17% return on pairs — below DCA, below transaction costs (stamp duty + fees). Short-selling restricted to designated securities list with tick-rule constraints. LTCM lost $4B when pairs broke. Solo operator can't absorb tail risk.

What IS useful: A-H spread as a sentiment signal. When same company trades at big premium/discount between Shanghai and HK, that's mainland vs international sentiment diverging. Worth watching in World Pulse, not worth trading directly.

Key refs: Gatev et al (2006) — seminal pairs trading paper. Zhang & Urquhart — HK/China pairs unprofitable. Li, Chui & Li (2014) — A-H cointegration exists but narrows post-Stock Connect 2014.

Verdict: Don't trade pairs in HK. Use A-H spread as context signal for World Pulse. DCA remains the proven strategy.
